## 👋 Hi there 

I’m Jorge, a software developer passionate about technology, problem-solving, and building meaningful digital experiences. I enjoy turning complex challenges into simple, scalable solutions while constantly looking for better ways to build, improve, and innovate.

Technology has always been there, even before it was my job title. Across 15 years in operations, service and business areas, I kept finding my way back to it: automating what was manual, rethinking processes and turning complex workflows into simpler, better ones. That’s where my passion for automation and process transformation was born.

Eventually, I went all in on software development. It was where I belonged. I brought with me something that’s hard to learn from documentation: understanding the business behind the code, communicating clearly, owning outcomes and caring about the impact of what gets built. Since then, I’ve kept expanding my skills across frontend, backend and modern development practices.

I enjoy digging into complex problems, understanding how things work and turning ideas into practical, maintainable solutions. I’m especially interested in how AI is changing the way we build software, always looking for new tools, better workflows and smarter ways to solve problems.
